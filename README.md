# Inverse energy cascade in self-gravitating collisionless dark matter flow and effects of halo shape

Xu Z., 2026, *Inverse energy cascade in self-gravitating collisionless dark matter flow and effects of halo shape*, Zenodo [doi:10.5281/zenodo.24094869](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24094869); the earlier version is hosted on arXiv as [arXiv:2110.13885](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.13885).

## Major findings

Einstein–de Sitter simulation of 256³ particles:

- The flux of any specific quantity V through the halo mass scale is ε<sub>V</sub> = −(μ + λ<sub>V</sub> − s) M<sub>h</sub> H⟨V⟩, where M<sub>h</sub> ∝ a<sup>μ</sup> is the mass in haloes, ⟨V⟩ ∝ a<sup>λ<sub>V</sub></sup> its mass-weighted mean and s the growth exponent of V in haloes of fixed mass; with the measured exponents it reproduces every energy flux to within 2 per cent at z &lt; 2.
- Kinetic energy, both of the motion of haloes and of the motion inside them, is carried to larger haloes (inverse cascade), while the inter- and intra-halo potential energies are carried to smaller haloes (direct cascade); the fluxes are independent of halo mass for haloes of up to a few hundred particles, only at z ≤ 0.3.
- The steady state of the cascade picture, in which M<sub>h</sub> ∝ a<sup>1/2</sup> and the energies grow as a<sup>3/2</sup>, is not reached by z = 0: M<sub>h</sub> grows as a<sup>1/2</sup> only near z = 0 (local exponent 0.47), and the total kinetic and potential energies grow as a<sup>1.13</sup> and a<sup>1.19</sup> at z ≈ 0.
- The simplest steady-state exponents fail for the inter-halo potential and for the radial kinetic energy of the haloes, so the measured exponents are needed in the flux formula.
- Haloes are elongated, with r<sub>λ3</sub>/r<sub>λ1</sub> ≈ 2–3, but their moment of inertia exceeds that of a sphere of equal volume only by a median factor 1.60 (1,674,583 haloes at z = 0).
- The spin axis lies within 30° of the plane perpendicular to the major axis for 70 per cent of haloes with n<sub>p</sub> ≥ 20 (isotropic: 50 per cent).
- Shape change therefore cannot drive the energy cascade; mass accretion does.
